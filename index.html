03 · Why we exist

Stay independent. Or sell on your terms.

Most independent operators in your size band eventually sell, usually to a private-equity-backed platform, and usually for less than they hoped. The reasons are predictable. Margins got thin. The business couldn't run a week without the owner. Both are admin problems in disguise.

If you want to stay independent for the long haul, we give you back the margin and the time to do it. If you decide to sell, we make the business worth more on the day a buyer asks for your books. Same work. Two outcomes. You pick.

Why now

What big companies have had for years. Now it's yours.

For years, this kind of automation only paid off for big companies. They had the budgets and the engineers to build it. That changed. The tools got cheaper and faster. The same capability the enterprise has run for years now fits a business your size. Most owners haven't caught up. The ones who move first get the margin back before their competitors do.

04 · How we work

It starts with a conversation.

We don't bill by the hour. We don't write slide decks. We build or use whatever software your workflow needs. Every engagement is scoped to your business, and it starts with you telling us what's eating your team's time.

First

A short call

You tell us what's costing your team time. We tell you, plainly, whether it's something we can fix. If it isn't, we say so and point you somewhere better. No pressure. No slides.

Then

We look at the work

We sit with the people doing it. Count the hours. Trace how the paperwork moves. You get a clear picture of what the admin work is costing you and what's worth fixing first.

Next

We fix one piece

We pick the task with the best return and automate it end to end, using the systems you already have. We test it against your actual work, and you see progress as it happens. No surprises at the end.

Ongoing

We keep it running, if you want us to

Tools change and processes drift. If you'd rather never think about the automation again, we watch for issues, tune things up when your tools or processes change, and fix what breaks.
